How to add page numbers to a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file.
  2. Pick the number position.
  3. Tap Add numbers and download the result.

Questions

What format are the numbers?
Page x / total, for example 3 / 12.

Can I start from a different number?
Not yet. Numbering starts at 1.
